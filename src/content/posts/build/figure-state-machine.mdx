A circuit breaker limits repeated calls to a slow or unavailable dependency. In **CLOSED**, requests pass through. After enough consecutive failures, **OPEN** rejects requests immediately. A timeout permits one probe in **HALF-OPEN**: success closes the breaker, while failure opens it again.

The strip beneath the state machine shows observed outcomes. As each point appears, the corresponding transition is highlighted above it.

<CircuitBreaker />

While the breaker is OPEN, requests do not reach the dependency and produce no outcome points. That gap is the recovery interval the breaker provides.
